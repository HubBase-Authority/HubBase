# Contributing to HubBase:
## Long-term:
### Long-term contributing means that:
1. You have a thing _(A new architecture, something else)_ and it is spanned across at least a **mini update** _(ex. 0.0.3.1 then 1 is a mini update)_
2. And you share it with HubBase Team.
## Short-term:
### Long-term contributing means that:
1. You have a thing _(A new program, small fix or something else)_ and it is spanned across at most a **2xmini update** _(ex. 0.0.3.1.25 then 2 is a 2xmini update)_
2. And you share it with HubBase Team.
## Common:
### Deadlines:
#### HubBase Team creates deadlines for creators, that have started, but did not yet finish their project for display on the [HubBase Wiki](https://github.com/HubBase-Authority/HubBase/wiki).
- If You miss a deadline, then you are given a 5-day grace period to complete your project. If you do not then you will be punished.
- Deadlines **can** be moved, but only **once**.
